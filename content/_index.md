Hey fellow data visualiser!

If you are new in town, welcome to Berlin! This website is intended to guide everyone, whether new or already involved in data visualisation, through the local scene. It’s an open list that was originally born out of the now inactive [Data Visualisation Meetup Berlin](https://luma.com/datavis-berlin). The list is maintained by [Jonas Parnow](https://jonasparnow.com).

If you have any additional questions or would like to participate or contribute to this [website](https://github.com/Data-Visualization-Berlin/Datavis-Berlin-Website), please feel free to get in touch via this [form](https://forms.gle/VUPMpCgor5LoazJS6), [mail](mailto:jonas@parnow.de) or [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/jonasparnow.com).

For more up-to-date information follow me on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/datavis.berlin), [Mastodon](https://vis.social/@berlin) or join this [Telegram Channel](https://t.me/visber).
