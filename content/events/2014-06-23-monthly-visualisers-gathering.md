  Hello visualisers, the end of June has come, time for our usual meetup! Get ready to gather on the Spree shore and chat about visualising and interacting with data, in a Summer chilled style. Last time someone wanted to bring some steaks in order to take advantage of the barbecue. Well, if the weather will be good again, you are free to do so! Beer will be already on site, laptops, drawing tools and ideas to exchange are on you!

  As always i invite you members to present us a little bit about your work. The group is huge and i saw very interesting self descriptions of people using R and D3 for example. I used Pandas but i never used R. Guys it would be great if you would like to share your work, some of you also have interesting and successful repositories on github, so why don't you take advantage of the wide communication bandwidth and show us some live data juggling? Or do you feel like presenting your favourite tools? Some design experiment? What is trivial for you may be revealing for others

  See you in one week!
