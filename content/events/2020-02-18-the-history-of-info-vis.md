  "Back to the Future – A (very, very) brief history of infovis"
  ---------------------------------
  As most #visber fans will know, the field of data visualisation (and information visualisation in general) has been exploding over the past ten or so years. We have brilliant people from all backgrounds working on a variety of challenges, and we have new tools and methods helping us to visualize ever more complex data sets.

  While everybody is looking forward to tackle the challenges of the future, there is only little integrated knowledge about the history of our field. This talk will present a brief journey back in time and provide a glimpse into the incredible wealth of our history: What were the main factors shaping infovis at different moments in time? Which were technologies and media supporting infovis back in the day? And which are the intellectual preferences we can find in old works?
