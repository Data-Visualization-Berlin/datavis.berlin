  We are back! After a very long break we have found a location for a new start of our **Datavis Meetup Berlin**.

  We would like to start with a round of introductions of people doing data visualisation in Berlin. And we start with **newsrooms**! We will have speakers from **Zeit Online**, **Tagesspiegel**, **Funke Interactive** and **Deutsche Welle Data**. They will talk about their workflows, projects, philosophies, problems and ideas.
  There will be time for questions after each presentation, and plenty of time later on to catch up on what everyone has been up to.

  Unfortunately, we are limited to **50 people**, so please make sure you only click on attend if you can come, or update your status accordingly.

  We will meet at the wonderful [CityLab Berlin](https://citylab-berlin.org/en/start/), located at **Platz der Luftbrücke 4, 12101 Berlin**. The entrance is on the left side of the building.
