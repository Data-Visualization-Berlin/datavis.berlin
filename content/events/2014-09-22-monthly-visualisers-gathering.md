description: |-
  Hi all, the meetup is going to happen in one week. If anybody has something to present, you are welcome to propose your idea! It does not have to be very structured or organised in slides, data visualisation varies a lot depending on the context and i think that many people have very different workflows, so talking about it and about the tools we use is always interesting. What is obvious for you may be revealing for someone else.

  As an alternative, you may propose an idea for us to hack on. It would be a nice way to learn together an try out our skills. That could be a project that is incomplete or stuck on some issue, the main requirement is that data should be public, in order for people to run their favourite machinery against it. I would love to start some effort about current global issues, or to discuss about it at least!

  Propose your ideas as comments below, we can decide counting the number of likes.

  Whether are we going to hack or not, you are encouraged to bring your tools, being them digital or analog (paper & markers)!
