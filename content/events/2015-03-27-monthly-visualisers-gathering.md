  Hello visionaries!

  Because our host c-base doesn't allow presentations on Mondays anymore, we will try something different and move the event to Friday. Join us for a drink to data-vis our way into the weekend! We are developers, designers, data scientists and other people interested in data vis, and we usually chat and gather around in a very informal way.

  This time, we will also experiment with lightning talks. So far, we have two of them:

  1. Daniel Kirsch (@kirel (https://twitter.com/kirel)) will talk about the recreation of chroropleth maps in R.

  http://photos1.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/8/4/f/f/600_435394047.jpeg

  2. Robert Penney will talk about Open Earth, a google earth style viewer with the ability to move up and down in time and view historical information and sources.

  http://photos1.meetupstatic.com/photos/event/8/4/f/5/600_435394037.jpeg

  If you want to present something, send an email to Lisa: lisacharlotterost@gmail.com. You're very welcome to talk 10 minutes or less about...

  • A visualisation project you're currently working on and for that you need some feedback, or requests for help for your own project

  • A project you've just finished and what you learned while working on it

  • Work done by others or technologies you want to introduce to others

  • Something else that other people might find interesting.

  Again, send an email to Lisa: lisacharlotterost@gmail.com

  Since "a picture is worth a thousand words", and since the idea of the meetup is to be very practical and hands-on, you are encouraged to bring your tools, being them digital or analog (paper & markers)!

  See you there!
